# speed-simulator

## Update — September 2026

**Fixes**
- Fixed saving. Progress on signed-in accounts wasn't being stored at all —
  levels, cores, upgrades and unlocked chassis now persist properly.
- Fixed the Crimson Wraith. It was impossible to genuinely outrun, which made
  the APEX challenge uncompletable. It can now be escaped by committing to your
  boost, and surviving a full chase finally counts.
- Fixed the game failing to load after the move to the new site.
- Removed the hazard rocks. Open space is clear again at any speed.

**Balance**
- Chassis unlock far sooner — the full roster now tops out at 42 prestiges
  instead of 120, and the world-bending chassis arrive much earlier.
- Boost lasts longer and recharges faster. It used to be unavailable for most
  of the early game.
- Getting caught by the Wraith hurts less: a smaller core loss and a shorter
  penalty.

**Visuals**
- The Classic Interceptor finally has a proper engine trail — a plume with
  shock cells and vortex rings that builds as you accelerate.
- Redesigned energy orbs. Clearer tiers you can read at a glance, and a
  consistent look across all of them.
- Collecting an orb now has real feedback, with a heavier hit on the big ones.
- Added depth behind the world — the simulation's lattice receding into the
  distance.

**Performance**
- Orb rendering is roughly 4x faster, with no loss in visual quality.
